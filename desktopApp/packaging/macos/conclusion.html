BitChord is installed

Open it from your Applications folder or with Spotlight, and sign in to the account whose library you want to see.

  • Command-comma opens Settings; Command-K jumps to search.
  • Your playlists, downloads and settings live in ~/Library/Application Support/BitChord.
  • Automix analyses tracks locally; the models ship with the app.

If macOS blocks the first launch because the build is unsigned, right-click the app and choose Open once.
